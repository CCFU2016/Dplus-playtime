Money needed

By purpose

Amounts are rounded to the nearest €1,000: they are estimates, not exact figures. All members sit in one wallet at one connected bank, and each member's need is worked out on its own: nothing nets between members (Direct+ contract).

The week, step by step

ECC collects each business day's trades once, by a transfer sent at 18:00 with value date the next business day; the money reaches ECC before 08:00. "Wallet needs" is what has to be in the available balance at that point, with your top-up setting.
